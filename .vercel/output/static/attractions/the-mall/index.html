The Whitgift Centre

Once one of the largest shopping centres in London. Now a fascinating study in slow urban decay, half-heartedly redevelopment plans, and the occasional still-open shop holding the fort.

Bring comfortable shoes and a sense of melancholy. Redevelopment has been "imminent" since roughly 2013.
