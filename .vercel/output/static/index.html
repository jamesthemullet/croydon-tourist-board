Welcome to Croydon.

Home of the world's largest tombstone, a decaying shopping mall, and the greatest concentration of fried chicken on Earth. Also: some genuinely good things. Come and see.

Official Attractions

Culinary

The Chicken Mile

The greatest concentration of fried chicken shops on planet Earth, condensed into a single stretch of High Street. A pilgrimage route for the discerning glutton.

Architecture

The Whitgift Centre

A shopping centre in the classical sense: it once contained shops. Now a monument to what Croydon used to buy. Bring a torch.

Architecture

The World's Largest Tombstone

Officially the Nestlé Tower / Norfolk House. Unofficially, the single most tombstone-shaped office block ever erected on a British high street. Make of that what you will.

Culture

Fairfield Halls

A genuinely lovely concert and arts venue, tastefully restored, and proof that not everything here is a cry for help.

Culinary

BoxPark Croydon

Shipping containers full of food, drink, and the unmistakable feeling that you are at a festival that never ends.
